A camera finding is not a replace-the-whole-line quote. See the pipe, then pick line, spot, or dig.

Chicago laterals are often clay or cast iron. See the line on camera after a jet before you buy a full replace or a liner.

A cloudy camera pass is not the same as a clear post-jet video. Spot repair, CIPP lining, and open-cut dig are different bids.

  • Ask for video after a jet, not only after a snake.
  • Ask which bid it is: spot repair, CIPP liner, pipe burst, or open-cut.
  • Ask for feet, depth, and what surface they cut.
  • Ask who pulls the City of Chicago sewer permit.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
