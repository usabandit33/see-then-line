Common questions

Does a camera mean I must replace the whole line?

No. Ask what the camera actually shows after the line is jetted, and how many feet they want to change.

Is lining the same as digging?

No. CIPP lining stays in the existing pipe. Open-cut removes and replaces pipe under the yard or pavement.

Do I need a city permit?

Often yes for sewer work in Chicago. Ask whose name is on the permit in the bid.
