Common questions

A Chicago sewer camera finding is not automatically a full-line replace. Ask for post-jet video, then choose spot, liner, or dig.

Does a camera mean I must replace the whole sewer line?

No. Ask for footage after a hydro-jet, how many feet of defect they see, and whether the bid is spot repair, CIPP liner, pipe bursting, or open-cut.

Is CIPP lining the same as digging up the sewer?

No. CIPP cures a liner inside the existing pipe. Open-cut digs up and replaces pipe under the yard or pavement.

Should I get a camera after jetting or only after snaking?

Ask for video after the line is clear enough to see. Snake-only and post-jet cameras are not the same inspection.

What is the difference between spot repair, CIPP, pipe bursting, and open-cut?

Spot changes a short section. CIPP lines the inside of the existing pipe. Pipe bursting pulls new pipe through the old path. Open-cut digs and replaces. Ask which method is on the written bid.

Do I need a City of Chicago sewer permit?

Often yes. Ask whether a permit is in the bid and whose name is on it before you sign.

Who owns the sewer lateral in Chicago?

On many properties the homeowner is responsible from the house to the city connection. Confirm for your address.

When is jetting enough without a liner or dig?

When the camera shows roots or grease that clear and the pipe structure looks intact. Collapse, large offsets, or long failed runs may need more than cleaning. Ask for the post-jet footage.

Does clay or cast iron change the decision?

Yes. Material matters. Ask what they saw on camera and which repair method fits that material.
